# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report it privately through GitHub:
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/dmatthan/typeset.js/security/advisories/new).
Do not open a public issue. Include what is affected, how to reproduce it, and its impact
as you understand it.

You will receive an acknowledgement within seven days. A confirmed vulnerability is fixed
in a release, and credited in its advisory unless you prefer otherwise.

## Scope

- The npm package `typeset.js` and its reader, as served by a site.
- The sharing service (`service/`), deployed at volumen.page.
- The web app at volumen.page and the desktop app.

Supported versions: the latest release. Fixes are not backported.
