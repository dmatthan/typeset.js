Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used and retained in connection with the Volumen website at volumen.page, the Volumen application available from it, and the associated sharing service (together, the “Service”). By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Books, highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading positions and settings (“Library Data”) are stored locally in your browser’s storage on your device, or in the files of the desktop application. Library Data is not transmitted to the Service unless you share a book or send notes on a book, as described below. You may export Library Data to a backup file, which remains solely in your possession.

3. Information Collected When a Book Is Shared

Before a book is shared, it is encrypted on the sharing writer’s device. The decryption key forms part of the link provided by the writer and is not transmitted to the Service. The Service stores the encrypted file, its size, the dates on which it was shared and on which it expires, and the identity of the writer who shared it. The Service is unable to access the contents or the title of a shared book.

4. Information Collected When Notes Are Sent

No account is required to read a shared book. Notes sent by a reader are encrypted on the reader’s device using a key derived from the same link, and are stored under an identifier specific to that book, such that notes sent by one reader on different books cannot be associated with one another. The name provided by the reader, any reactions, and reading progress (unless the reader disables this) are contained within the encrypted notes. Encrypted notes are made available only to the signed-in writer who shared the book. The Service is unable to access their contents.

5. Information Collected from Writers

Writer accounts are available by invitation only. A writer account consists of the name provided by the writer and the public key of each passkey used to sign in; passkeys themselves remain on the writer’s devices. Where the writer’s passkey supports it, the Service also stores the writer’s list of shares, encrypted with a key generated by the passkey on the writer’s devices, which is not transmitted to the Service.

6. Cookies

The Service sets a single cookie, solely to keep a writer signed in, for a period of thirty days. The Service does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Service Providers

The Service is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. (“Cloudflare”), which processes the network address of each visitor for the purposes of delivering the Service and preventing abuse. Cloudflare Turnstile is used to verify that a person is present when an invitation is accepted, when a writer signs in, and on the first occasion that a reader sends notes on a book. The use of these services is subject to Cloudflare’s own privacy policy. Fonts are served from the Service itself and not from any third party.

8. Use of Network Addresses

The Service counts requests per network address over periods of one minute in order to limit abuse. The Service does not retain records of network addresses or of the requests made.

9. Data Retention

A shared book, together with all notes sent on it, is deleted when the writer withdraws the link, or ninety days after the date on which it was shared unless the writer extends this period. Library Data is retained on your device until you delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of the Service following any update constitutes acknowledgement of the revised Policy.

11. Contact

Questions regarding this Policy may be raised through the project’s repository at github.com/dmatthan/typeset.js.
