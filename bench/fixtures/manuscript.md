# Chapter One: The Harbour

The lantern had burned for *forty years* before anyone thought to ask who kept it.[^1] Mara asked on a Tuesday.

Her father laughed, as he laughed at most questions, and said the keeper was **nobody** at all.

> A light that no one keeps is still a light, until it isn't.

She wrote that down in the back of her schoolbook, under the sums.

* * *

By morning the harbour board had sent a letter.[^2]

# Chapter Two: The Stair

The stair turned eleven times. Mara counted, and then counted again, because the second time there were twelve.

- a lamp with no oil

- a chair facing the sea

Nothing else was in the room.

[^1]: Some say fifty; the harbour records were lost in the flood.
[^2]: It was signed by three men, one of whom could not write.
